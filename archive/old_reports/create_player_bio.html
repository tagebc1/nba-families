Player bio construction

This reproducible report creates only the player-level data/player_bio.csv from the three approved inputs. It deliberately does not read salary values into the output, and does not use family files, height/weight, or the prior merging script.

The script uses common_player_info as the authoritative player register. Unmatched names from the season and salary sources are excluded from player_bio.csv and recorded in the consolidated review file. Suffixes are preserved in output names; only an internal normalized key removes them. School selection is the first deterministic non-missing all_seasons$college value, then common_player_info$school. Missing birthdates are reported but are not treated as identity conflicts.
